Constitutional Amendments

Key changes to the Constitution since 1950, with brief overviews.

1st Amendment (1951)

Abolished zamindari, added land reforms, and restricted freedom of speech for public order.

View PDF

7th Amendment (1956)

Reorganized states on linguistic basis, creating new states like Andhra Pradesh.

View PDF

24th Amendment (1971)

Abolished privy purses for former rulers and nationalized banks.

View PDF

42nd Amendment (1976)

Added Fundamental Duties, changed preamble to "secular democratic republic," and strengthened central powers.

View PDF

44th Amendment (1978)

Restored fundamental rights, limited emergency powers, and protected property rights.

View PDF

73rd Amendment (1992)

Granted constitutional status to Panchayats for local self-governance.

View PDF

86th Amendment (2002)

Made education a fundamental right for children aged 6-14.
